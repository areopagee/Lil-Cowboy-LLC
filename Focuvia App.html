Focuvia Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026  |  App version 2.1

In short

Focuvia is an offline study planner. It does not collect, send or share your personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

1. Who we are

Focuvia is developed by Lil Cowboy Team (“we”, “us”). You can contact us at contact@lilcowboy.space.

2. Information you enter in the app

To work, Focuvia stores the information you add yourself:

  • Subjects, including names, colors, icons, weekly targets, exam dates and study materials
  • Tasks, including titles, due dates and times, priorities, categories and reminder settings
  • Focus sessions, study minutes, distraction counts, streaks and statistics
  • Your settings, such as timer lengths and daily and weekly goals

This information is saved only in the app’s private storage on your device. We cannot see it, and it is not uploaded to any server.

3. What we do not collect

  • No account, sign-up or login is required
  • No name, email address, phone number or location
  • No device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools
  • No ads and no third-party SDKs

Focuvia does not request any Android permissions and does not connect to the internet.

4. Features you control

Export statistics (CSV). If you choose to export, Android’s file picker lets you pick where the file is saved. The file is created only where you choose, and we do not receive it.

Share weekly report. If you tap Share, the text of your report is passed to the Android share sheet. It goes only to the app you select, and that app’s own privacy policy applies from then on.

In-app reminders. Reminders are shown inside the app only. Focuvia does not send notifications.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, share or disclose your data to anyone, because we never receive it.

6. Device backups

Your Android device may include app data in its own backup service (for example, a Google account backup) if you have turned that feature on in your device settings. This is controlled by Android and by you, not by Focuvia. Backed-up data is handled under the policy of your backup provider.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. You can delete it at any time:

  • Open Settings in the app and choose Delete all data, or
  • Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings.

Because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot recover it for you once it is deleted.

8. Children’s privacy

Focuvia collects no personal information from anyone, including children. It has no accounts, ads or online features.

9. Security

Your data is stored in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot normally access. Since the app has no network access, your data is not transmitted from the app. Keeping your device locked and up to date helps protect it further.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at this page. If a future version of Focuvia collects any data, we will update this policy before that change is released.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email contact@lilcowboy.space.
