How we work

A short, predictable process, so you always know what happens next.

What we ask from you

  • A short description of the tasks you want to hand over.
  • Access to the tools or shared folders we will use.
  • A named person who can answer our questions.

What you get

  • A written plan with tasks and timelines.
  • Regular progress updates.
  • Changes to the plan whenever your needs change.
  1. Discovery call. Tell us what slows you down.
  2. Plan. We agree tasks, tools and timelines in writing.
  3. Onboard. We set up access and take over the first tasks.
  4. Deliver. We carry out the work and keep you updated.
  5. Review. We check results with you and adjust the plan.
