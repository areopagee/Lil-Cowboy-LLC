Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026  •  Last updated: October 14, 2026

The short version

  • QR Code Cowboy works fully offline. The app has no internet access.
  • We do not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal data.
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Your text and settings stay on your device unless you choose to share them.

1. Who we are

QR Code Cowboy (the “App”) is developed and operated by Lil Cowboy LLC (“we”, “us”, “our”). This Privacy Policy explains what happens to information when you use the App.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App has no user accounts, contains no advertising or analytics software, and does not request internet permission, so it cannot send data to us or to anyone else.

3. Information stored on your device

To work properly, the App keeps the following on your device only. We never receive it.

4. How the App's features use your data

  • Generating QR codes: Your text is turned into a QR code locally on your device.
  • Reading QR codes from a photo: You pick an image using the Android system photo picker. The App only receives the image you select, reads it on your device, and does not upload it. It is not saved by the App and is discarded when you close the App.
  • Clipboard: The App reads the clipboard only when you tap “Paste”, and writes to it only when you tap “Copy”.
  • Sharing: When you tap “Share”, Android's share menu opens and the text or image goes to the app you choose. That app's own privacy policy then applies. We do not see or control where you share.

5. Permissions

  • The App does not request internet, camera, location, contacts, microphone or account permissions.
  • On Android 10 and newer, no permissions are needed. Saving images uses Android's standard media storage.
  • On Android 9 and older only, Android requires the “Storage” permission to save images to your gallery. It is used only when you tap “Save QR”.

6. Third-party code

The App uses the open-source ZXing library to create and read QR codes. It runs entirely on your device and does not communicate with any server. The App contains no third-party advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

7. Data sharing and sale

Because we do not collect your data, we do not sell, rent, trade or share it with anyone.

8. Data security

Your data stays on your device, protected by Android's app sandbox and your device's own security. Because nothing is transmitted, there is no server-side data for us to lose. Please be careful what you put into QR codes you share publicly, since anyone who scans a code can read its contents.

9. Your rights and choices

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. You control everything the App stores: clear the text box, switch off “Remember last message”, delete saved images, clear the App's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the App. Rights you may have under laws such as the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA are respected by this design, and you can contact us with any questions.

10. Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13 (or under 16 in some regions). It is safe for general audiences because it collects nothing.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the App adds new features. The updated version will be posted here with a new “Last updated” date. If a change affects how your data is handled, we will make that clear in the App or its store listing.
